A cozy sanctuary with a colour-changing house tea

Tucked along Baimal Marga, Brew Butterfly Cafe is where Kathmandu's vibrant food culture meets a relaxed lounge atmosphere — serving hot spiced milk tea in the morning, wok-tossed chowmein through the afternoon, and cloud hookahs after dark.

Our pride is the house pour — Butterfly Pea Tea, brewed indigo-blue from organic pea flowers, shifting into royal purple when fresh lemon juice meets the cup.
